The Official YouTube Thumbnail Dimensions

The recommended YouTube thumbnail size in 2026 is 1280 × 720 pixels at a 16:9 aspect ratio. This has been consistent for several years and remains unchanged. YouTube accepts a minimum width of 640 pixels, but uploading at the minimum will result in visible blurriness on larger screens. Always design and upload at the full 1280×720.

Design tip: Some creators design at 1280×760 to account for YouTube's UI overlays on the top and bottom edges. The extra 40 pixels of height gives you a small buffer so nothing important gets clipped.

File Format and Size Requirements

YouTube accepts thumbnails in the following formats:

  • JPG / JPEG — best choice for photographs and complex images; compresses well under the 2MB limit
  • PNG — best for designs with text, flat colors, or transparency needs; slightly larger file size
  • GIF — supported but not animated; thumbnails display as static images
  • BMP — supported but rarely used; very large file sizes

The maximum file size is 2MB for video thumbnails and 10MB for podcast thumbnails. JPG at around 85–90% quality is the sweet spot — sharp enough to look professional, small enough to stay under the limit comfortably.

The Safe Zone — Where NOT to Put Important Elements

YouTube overlays the video duration in the bottom-right corner of every thumbnail. If you place text, faces, or key visual elements in that area, they will be partially covered. As a rule, keep the bottom-right 15–20% of the image free of anything important.

Additionally, on mobile, thumbnails render as small as approximately 120 pixels wide in the suggested video feed. At that size, fine details disappear. Design with bold shapes, high contrast, and minimal text so the thumbnail communicates clearly even at thumbnail size.

YouTube Shorts Thumbnail Size

YouTube Shorts uses a vertical 9:16 format rather than the standard 16:9. If you upload a horizontal (16:9) custom thumbnail for a Shorts video, YouTube automatically crops it to a 4:5 ratio on the home page and explore page. To avoid unexpected cropping, design Shorts thumbnails with a vertical-safe composition — keep your subject centered and avoid placing important elements near the left or right edges.

The recommended size for Shorts thumbnails is 1080 × 1920 pixels at a 9:16 aspect ratio. The same 2MB file size limit applies.

Does Thumbnail Size Affect CTR?

Directly, no — YouTube displays thumbnails at fixed sizes in the interface regardless of the resolution you upload. Indirectly, yes — uploading at the correct full resolution ensures your thumbnail looks sharp and professional on all screen sizes, including 4K TVs and high-DPI displays. A blurry thumbnail signals low quality to viewers before they even read the title.

Research in 2026 consistently shows that 90% of top-performing YouTube videos use custom thumbnails. The size itself isn't what drives clicks — but the quality and design made possible by uploading at the right dimensions certainly does.

How to Enable Custom Thumbnails on Your Channel

Before you can upload a custom thumbnail, your YouTube channel must be verified with a phone number. Go to YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility and complete the phone verification. It takes under a minute and unlocks the custom thumbnail option on every video upload and edit screen.
